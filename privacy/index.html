Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This policy explains what data Flightcraft collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Who We Are

Flightcraft is a mobile airline tycoon game developed by Stratos Labs. This privacy policy applies to the Flightcraft Android app and related services.

Data We Collect

We collect game-related data needed to operate the game, including your airline name, cash, routes, hubs, fleet, game day, achievements, leaderboard score, and saved progress.

We also collect your Google Play Games player ID and display name to identify your account, save progress, and operate leaderboard features. We may collect device type, OS version, and basic crash or diagnostic information.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, or precise location.

How We Use Data

We use your data solely to save and restore your game progress, operate leaderboard features, process in-app purchases, deliver rewarded ads, and maintain app stability.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Leaderboard

If you use the leaderboard, your airline name and score may be visible to other players. Your airline name is chosen by you and does not need to be your real name.

Storage and Security

Game data is stored on your device and in Supabase, a cloud database provider. Your account is identified by your Google Play Games player ID — no password is stored by Flightcraft.

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

Advertising

Flightcraft includes optional rewarded video ads powered by Google AdMob. These ads are shown only when you choose to watch them in exchange for in-game rewards. You are never shown ads without opting in.

AdMob may use your advertising ID to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings under Privacy → Ads.

Notifications

Flightcraft may use local notifications to remind you about game progress, rewards, or leaderboard changes. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

Flightcraft uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Games Services — authentication and leaderboards
  • Supabase — encrypted cloud game data storage
  • Google AdMob — opt-in rewarded video ads
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase processing
  • Sentry — anonymous crash reporting
  • Expo / EAS — app distribution and updates

Each service operates under its own privacy policy.

Children

Flightcraft is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your Choices and Rights

You may delete your game data at any time using Delete Account in Settings. This permanently removes your airline and all progress from our servers.

You can opt out of personalised ads through your Google account settings or device advertising preferences.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make important changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and may notify you through the app store listing.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through the Flightcraft page on the Google Play Store.
